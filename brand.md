# Clariva — Brand Reference

> Source: `Clariva_Brand_Strategy_1.docx` + logo image (`Clariva_Brand_Strategy_1.png`)

---

## Product Name

**Clariva** — from Latin *clarus* (clear, bright, illustrious) + *-iva* (life, movement). Meaning: illuminated clarity in motion.

- **Master brand**: Clariva
- **B2B tier**: Clariva for Organisations
- **Personal tier**: Clariva Personal

---

## Category

**Mission Financial Stewardship Software (MFSS)** — sits between lightweight bookkeeping tools and enterprise ERPs. Built for NGOs, churches, ministries, and mission-driven organizations.

---

## Brand Essence

**Trusted Clarity** — every design and copy decision must pass this filter:
- *Trusted* — does this make users feel safe, confident, accountable?
- *Clarity* — does this make financial information simple, visible, understandable?

---

## Brand Promise

> We make it easy for mission-driven organizations to handle money with the integrity it deserves.

---

## Positioning Statement

> For mission-driven organizations that hold money in trust — NGOs, churches, ministries, and projects — Clariva is the financial stewardship platform that replaces spreadsheets and generic accounting tools with a purpose-built system for accountability, transparency, and trust.

## Tone of Voice

- **Overall**: Warm authority. Knowledgeable without being superior.
- **Onboarding**: Welcoming, patient. "You're in good hands. Let's set this up together."
- **Product UI**: Clear, concise, action-oriented. "Your reconciliation is complete. 3 items need review."
- **Error states**: Calm, solution-first. "Something looks off here. Here's how to fix it."
- **Reports**: Professional, precise. "Your financial position is clear and ready to share."

**Voice Rules**
- Never use accounting jargon without plain-English translation
- Lead with benefit before feature
- Use "your organization" not "the organization"
- Active voice always
- Avoid fear-based messaging

## Brand Values

1. Integrity — default toward transparency
2. Stewardship — treat users' trust as they treat donors' money
3. Simplicity — complexity is disrespect for users' time
4. Empowerment — give every org the confidence of enterprise tools
5. Purpose — test every feature: does this help mission orgs do more good?
