## What is this chapter about?

When your organisation is brand new, the app asks a few questions first (the **onboarding wizard**). After that, you build your "building blocks" on the **Setup** page: banks, categories, income types, and more. This chapter walks through both.

## Part 1: The onboarding wizard (3 easy steps)

You see this right after creating a new organisation.

1. **Step 1 — Organisation Details.** Type your organisation's name in **Organisation Name** and click **Next**.
2. **Step 2 — Financial Setup.** Pick your **Default Currency** (the money you use every day), the month your **Financial Year Starts In**, and your **Timezone**. Then click **Next**.
3. **Step 3 — Ready.** Check the summary. If everything looks right, click **Enter Application**. Don't worry — you can change these later in Setup.

## Part 2: The Setup page

Only **Admins** and **Accountants** can open Setup. Find it in the left menu under **Administration → Setup**. It has tabs across the top: **General**, **Banks**, **Distribution Rules**, **Special Rules**, **Income Types**, **Outflow Types**, **Departments**, **Currencies**.

### How to add a bank

1. Click the **Banks** tab.
2. Click **+ Add Bank**.
3. Type the **Bank Name** (example: First Bank).
4. You can also type the **Account Number**, pick an **Account Type** (Current, Savings, Fixed Deposit, Domiciliary) and the **Account Currency**.
5. If the bank already has money in it, type that money in **Starting Balance**.
6. If you typed a starting balance, the app asks how to split it between funds. Choose **Percentage %** or **Amount**, click **+ Add row**, and for each row pick a Fund, a Portion, and a value. The totals must add up (100% or the full starting balance) — the app shows green when it's right.
7. Click **Add Bank**. Done! The bank now appears everywhere in the app.

### How to add an income type

Income types are name tags for money coming in, like "Tithes" or "Offerings".

1. Click the **Income Types** tab, then **+ Add Income Type**.
2. Type a **Name** (example: Sunday Tithe) and pick a **Color**.
3. Optional but clever: add **Recognition Rules**. Click **+ Add rule**, choose **Keyword**, and type a word like "tithe". From now on, when a bank statement line contains that word, the app guesses this income type by itself.
4. Click **Add Income Type**.

### How to add an outflow type

Outflow types are name tags for money going out, like "Salaries" or "Utilities".

1. Click the **Outflow Types** tab, then **+ Add Outflow Type**.
2. Type a **Name**, pick a **Color**, and tick any **Linked Categories** it usually belongs to.
3. Click **Add Type**.

### How to add a department

1. Click the **Departments** tab, then **+ Add Department**.
2. Type a **Name** (example: Welfare). You can add a short **Code** (example: WLF) and a **Description**.
3. Leave **Active** ticked, and click **Add Department**.

### How to change the accounting year

1. Click the **General** tab.
2. Under **Accounting Year**, click the year you want to work in.
3. Click **Save**. All pages and reports now show that year.

### How to add another currency

1. Click the **Currencies** tab.
2. Fill in **Code** (like CHF), **Symbol** (like Fr.), **Name** (like Swiss Franc), and an optional flag emoji.
3. Click **+ Add Currency**.

### What about the Distribution Rules and Special Rules tabs?

Those control how incoming money is split between categories. They are explained fully in Chapter 11 (Distribution Rules).

## What you'll see when setup is done

A checklist on the Dashboard ticks itself off as you finish each task. When everything is done it says **Setup complete!** and disappears.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Name is required"** — you left the name box empty. Type a name.
- **The allocation totals are amber, not green** — your split doesn't add up to 100% (or to the starting balance). Fix the numbers.
- **You can't see Setup at all** — your role is Viewer. Ask an Admin to do it, or to change your role.
