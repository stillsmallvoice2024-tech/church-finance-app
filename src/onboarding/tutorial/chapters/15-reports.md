## How to Generate a Monthly Report

**What you'll need:** Imported transactions for the year you want to review.

**Where to go:** Reports → Reports → Monthly Breakdown tab

**Time:** ~3 minutes.

---

### Steps

1. Open **Reports → Reports** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Monthly Breakdown** tab across the top of the page.
3. Use the **Year** selector to choose the year.
4. The table shows each month with its total inflows, total outflows, and net balance.
5. To export the table as a spreadsheet, click the **CSV** button at the top right of the tab.

![Screenshot: the Monthly Breakdown tab with a year selected and months listed with totals](./screenshots/15-reports-step-05.png)

### Result

You have a month-by-month summary of all financial activity for the year, ready to share or file.

---

### Common issues

- **All months show zero:** No transactions have been imported for that year yet. Import statements via Daily Finance → Import first.
- **Totals look incorrect:** Verify that all statements for the year have been imported and that the correct bank was selected during each import.

## How to Generate an Annual Summary

**Where to go:** Reports → Reports → Annual Summary tab

### Steps

1. Open **Reports → Reports**.
2. Click the **Annual Summary** tab.
3. The table lists every year on record with its total inflows, outflows, and net balance.
4. Click **CSV** to export.

## How to View Income and Outflow Breakdowns

**Where to go:** Reports → Reports → Income Type Breakdown or Outflow Type Breakdown tab

### Steps

1. Open **Reports → Reports**.
2. Click **Income Type Breakdown** to see how much each income category brought in, with percentage shares and a progress bar.
3. Click **Outflow Type Breakdown** to see the same for expenditure categories.
4. Use the **Year** and **Month** selectors at the top to filter the period.
5. Click **CSV** to export the breakdown.

## How to Build a Financial Report Layout

**What you'll need:** Categories configured with transactions. Useful for board presentations and formal statements.

**Where to go:** Reports → Financial Report

**Time:** ~10 minutes the first time; instant once saved as a template.

---

### Steps

1. Open **Reports → Financial Report**.
2. Click **Edit Layout**.
3. Click **Add Table** and give it a title (click the title text to rename it — e.g. "Income").
4. Click **+ Group** on the table and name the group (e.g. "Offerings").
5. In the **Add Rows** panel on the left, search for a category, choose its portion, pick the target group, and click it to add it as a line item.
6. Repeat for all your lines. Drag the grip handles to reorder rows. Use the eye icon to temporarily hide a line. Use **+ Subgroup** for nested groupings.
7. Click **Done Editing** to see the finished report with sub-totals and a grand total.
8. Pick the **Date** at the top to view balances as of any day.
9. Click **Save Template**, type a name, and click **Save Template** again to reuse this layout later.
10. To export: click **PDF** or **Excel** at the top right of the view.

### Result

A formatted financial statement with live balances, sub-totals, and a grand total — ready for leadership review or official filing.

## How to Create a Custom Report

**Where to go:** Reports → Custom Reports

**Time:** ~5 minutes.

### Steps

1. Open **Reports → Custom Reports**.
2. Click **New Report** and type a name for the report.
3. Add building blocks with the **Add block** buttons: **Text** (prose), **Metric** (one number), **Table** (a category summary), or **Formula** (a calculated total).
4. Inside a Text block, click **Insert** to embed a live number inline in a sentence.
5. Click **Preview** to see the report with real data, then **Save**.
6. Use **Share** to copy a link, **Print** to print, or open the **Snapshots** panel to save a frozen copy of the numbers at a point in time.
