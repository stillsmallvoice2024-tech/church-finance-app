## What is this chapter about?

The app has three report pages, all under **Reports** in the left menu:

- **Reports** — ready-made summaries (yearly, monthly, by income type, and more).
- **Financial Report** — a builder for your official financial statement layout.
- **Custom Reports** — free-form documents with live numbers inside.

## Part 1: The Reports page

Find it at **Reports → Reports**. Tabs across the top switch between summaries:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Annual Summary | Total in, total out, and net for each year |
| Monthly Breakdown | The same, month by month (pick the year) |
| Income Type Breakdown | How much each income type brought in, with % shares |
| Outflow Type Breakdown | How much each spending type cost, with % shares |
| Departments | Spending per department — click a row to see its transactions |
| FX Holdings | Foreign currency balances |
| Audit Log | Who changed what (admins only) |

Every tab has a **CSV** button to download the table, and the page has a **Print** button at the top.

## Part 2: The Financial Report builder

Find it at **Reports → Financial Report**. This builds a formal statement — tables with groups, sub-totals, and grand totals — using live balances on a date you choose.

### How to build your first report

1. Click **Edit Layout**.
2. Click **Add Table** and give it a title (click the title to rename it — for example "Income").
3. Click **+ Group** on the table and name the group (for example "Offerings").
4. In the **Add Rows** panel on the left, search for a category, choose its portion, pick the target group, and click it to add it as a line.
5. Repeat for all your lines. Drag the grip handles to reorder; use the eye icon to hide a line; use **+ Subgroup** for lines inside lines.
6. Click **Done Editing**. You now see the finished report with all sub-totals and a grand total.
7. Pick the **Date** at the top to see balances as of any day.

### How to save and reuse the layout

1. While editing, click **Save Template**, type a name (example: "Monthly Board Report"), and click **Save Template**.
2. Later, open the **Templates** dropdown and click the template to load it. Click the **pin** icon to make it load automatically every time.

### How to export

In view mode, click **PDF** or **Excel** at the top right. The file downloads immediately.

## Part 3: Custom Reports

Find it at **Reports → Custom Reports**. These are documents you write yourself — with living numbers that update automatically.

### How to create one

1. Click **New Report**. The editor opens.
2. Click the title at the top and name your report.
3. Add building blocks with the **Add block** buttons at the bottom: **Text** (write sentences), **Metric** (one big number, like a category balance), **Table** (a category summary table), **Formula** (a calculation that adds and subtracts balances).
4. Inside a Text block, click **Insert** to drop a live number right inside your sentence.
5. Click **Preview** to see it with real numbers, then **Save**.

### Extra powers

- **Share** — copies a link to the report.
- **Print** — prints the preview.
- **Snapshots** — freeze the numbers at a moment in time (example: "May 2026 month-end"). Open the **Snapshots** panel, type a label, and click **Save**. You can view the snapshot later even after the live numbers change.

## If something goes wrong

- **"No layout configured"** on the Financial Report — click **Edit Layout** and add a table first.
- **A number looks wrong** — check the date at the top, and check the line's portion (All, Percentage, Specific Seed, Savings).
- **"No reports yet"** on Custom Reports — click **New Report** to create your first one.
