## What are Categories?

A category is a bucket for money — like "General Fund", "Building Fund", or "Welfare". Money in and money out can be tagged with a category, so you always know which bucket it belongs to. This chapter covers two pages: **Categories** (where you create the buckets) and **Category Accounts** (where you watch what's inside them).

## Part 1: The Categories page

Find it in the left menu: **Budget & Allocation → Categories**. There are two tabs at the top: **Local** (your everyday currency) and **FX** (foreign-currency categories).

### How to create a category

1. Click **Add Category**.
2. Type the **Category Name** (example: Welfare).
3. Optional: pick a **Group** to keep similar categories together (example: a "Ministries" group). Click the small folder-plus icon to create a new group right there.
4. Optional: type a **Description**.
5. Optional: add **Opening Balances by Portion** — money the bucket already had before you started using the app. Click **Add**, pick a portion (Percentage Allocation, Specific Seed, or Savings), and type the amount.
6. Click **Create**.

### How to edit, hide, or delete a category

1. Find the category card or row.
2. Click the **pencil** to edit, then **Save Changes**.
3. Click the **trash** to delete. If the category already has transactions, the app will not delete it — instead it offers **Hide Category**. Hidden categories disappear from lists but keep their history.
4. To bring back a hidden category: click **Show hidden (N)** at the top, find the category, and click the **eye** icon.

## Part 2: The Category Accounts page

Find it at **Budget & Allocation → Category Accounts**. It has three views: **Summary**, **Ledger**, and **FX**.

### The Summary view

This shows totals for every category in three pockets:

| Pocket | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Regular Funds | Money split by your percentage rules |
| Designated Gifts | Money given for one special purpose |
| Savings Balance | Money set aside as a reserve |

Four cards at the top show the grand totals. Use the buttons (**All**, **Regular Funds**, **Designated Gifts**, **Savings**) to filter, or pick one category from the dropdown.

### The Ledger view (every single transaction)

1. Click the **Ledger** tab.
2. Pick a portion: **Percentage**, **Specific Seed**, or **Savings**.
3. Pick a category from the dropdown.
4. You now see every transaction for that bucket, with a running **Balance** column. The first row is **Balance Brought Forward** — the opening balance.
5. Rows that say "Transfer →" or "Transfer ←" are internal moves between buckets (Chapter 10). Click the arrow to expand and see details.

### The FX view

Pick a currency to see all its foreign-currency transactions with a running balance.

## If something goes wrong

- **"No categories yet"** — click **Add Category** to create your first one.
- **Cannot delete a category** — it has transactions. Hide it instead.
- **Share % column shows "—"** — no distribution rule is active today. See Chapter 11.
