## What is this chapter about?

Sometimes money needs to move between buckets — for example, from the "General Fund" bucket to the "Building Fund" bucket. No money enters or leaves the organisation; it just changes pockets. That's a **Fund-to-Fund Transfer**. When you need to move money for MANY funds at once, use **Bulk Reallocation**.

Find both at **Daily Finance → Fund-to-Fund Transfer**. The page has two tabs: **Fund-to-Fund Transfer** and **Bulk Reallocation**.

## Part 1: One transfer at a time

### How to record a transfer

1. Click **Add Transfer**.
2. Pick the **Date** and type the **Amount**.
3. Pick the **From Fund** (where the money leaves) and the **From Fund Type** (which pocket: **Regular Funds**, **Designated Gift**, or **Savings Funds**).
4. Pick the **To Fund** and the **To Fund Type**.
5. Type a **Description** (example: "Move surplus to Building Fund").
6. Optional: add a **Transaction Ref** and a **Remark**.
7. Click **Save Transfer**.

The transfer now shows in both funds' ledgers (Chapter 7): as money out of one and money into the other.

### Finding, editing, and deleting transfers

- Filter by dates, **From Fund**, or **To Fund**, or use the search box.
- Click the **pencil** to edit a transfer, the **trash** to delete it.
- Tick several rows to use **Edit selected** or **Delete selected**.

## Part 2: Bulk Reallocation (move money for many funds at once)

Example: at year end, you want to move half of every fund's Regular Funds into Savings. Instead of typing dozens of transfers, do it in one go.

### How to do a bulk reallocation

1. Click the **Bulk Reallocation** tab.
2. Pick the **Source Fund Type** (where money comes from) and the **Destination Fund Type** (where it goes). They must be different.
3. Pick the **Mode**: **Full Balance** (move everything), **Percentage** (move a share, like 50%), or **Fixed Amount** (move the same amount from each).
4. In the table, tick the funds you want to include (or tick the box in the header for all). The **Amount to Move** column shows exactly what will move for each.
5. Check the summary cards: **Selected** and **Total to Move**.
6. Click **Preview**.
7. On the preview screen, check the table. Amber **Zero after** badges warn you that a fund's source pocket will be emptied. Set the **Date** and adjust the **Description** if you like.
8. Click **Confirm — N Transfer(s)**.

The app creates one transfer per fund and shows: "Bulk reallocation complete".

## If something goes wrong

- **"Source and destination fund types must differ"** — you picked the same fund type twice; change one.
- **"N funds were skipped"** — those funds had nothing to move. That's okay.
- **You made a mistake** — every bulk run is just a set of normal transfers. Go to the Fund-to-Fund Transfer tab, find them (same date and description), and edit or delete them.
