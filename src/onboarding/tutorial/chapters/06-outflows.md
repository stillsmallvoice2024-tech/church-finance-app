## What is the Outflows page?

Outflows are money going OUT — salaries, bills, purchases, and any spending. This page lists every outflow so you can search, check, fix, and export them. Find it in the left menu: **Daily Finance → Outflows**.

It works almost exactly like the Inflows page (Chapter 5), with a few extras explained below.

## What's extra on this page

- Two more filters: a **Fund** dropdown and an **Outflow Type** dropdown, so you can see, for example, only "Utilities" spending.
- Amounts are shown in red and the column is called **Amount Disbursed**.
- Some rows show extra badges: **Pending** (approved but money hasn't left the bank yet), **Root** (an original transaction), **Offset** (a linked correction like a refund).
- A paper-clip column shows when a receipt file is attached.
- If a row was partly refunded, you may see a **NET** amount — what it really cost after refunds and charges.

## How to find spending for one fund

1. Open the **Fund** dropdown in the filter card and pick a fund.
2. If you also want one kind of spending, pick it in the **Outflow Type** dropdown.
3. The summary strip updates to show the total for what you filtered.

## How to edit one outflow

1. Click the **pencil** button on the row.
2. The **Edit Outflow Transaction** window opens. You can change the **Date**, **Amount Disbursed**, **Bank Account**, **Description**, **Fund**, **Fund Type**, **Outflow Type (reporting)**, and **Department / Unit**.
3. Tick or untick **Mark as Pending Deduction** if the payment status changed.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

## What do Fund, Fund Type, and Outflow Type mean?

- **Fund** — which money bucket pays for it (example: Welfare).
- **Fund Type** — which pocket inside that bucket: **Regular Funds**, **Designated Gift**, or **Savings**. (Chapter 11 and 12 explain these.)
- **Outflow Type** — a reporting label like Salaries or Utilities. It doesn't move money; it just helps reports group your spending.

## Bulk edit, delete, and export

These work exactly like on the Inflows page: tick rows → **Edit selected** or **Delete selected**; use **Export** for spreadsheets.

## If something goes wrong

- **Total looks too big** — check the **Effective Total** box (amber). It shows the total after refunds/offsets are removed.
- **A payment shows but the money hasn't left the bank** — that's a Pending Deduction. See Chapter 12 for how to resolve it when the bank debits it.
- **You can't edit** — your role is Viewer.
