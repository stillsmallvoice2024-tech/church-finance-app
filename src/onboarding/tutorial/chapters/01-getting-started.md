## What is this chapter about?

This chapter shows you how to get into the app for the very first time: creating an account, signing in, fixing a forgotten password, and joining an organisation that invited you.

## Before you start

- You need the web address (link) of the app. Your organisation administrator can give it to you.
- You need an email address you can open.

## How to sign in (you already have an account)

1. Open the app link in your browser. You will see the sign-in page.
2. Find the box labelled **Email or Username**. Type your email address (or your username, if you made one).
3. Find the box labelled **Password**. Type your password. Tip: click the little eye icon to see what you typed.
4. Click the big **Sign in** button.
5. If your account uses two-step security (2FA), a small window appears asking for a 6-digit code. Open your authenticator app on your phone (like Google Authenticator), find the code, type it in, and click **Verify**.
6. You will land on the **Dashboard** — the home page of the app.

## How to create a brand-new organisation (first person from your organisation)

Only do this if your organisation does NOT already use the app. If your organisation already uses it, ask an admin to invite you instead (see "How to join by invitation" below).

1. On the sign-in page, click the link that says **Create a new organisation**.
2. In **Organisation Name**, type your organisation's name. Example: Grace Community Church.
3. In **Full Name**, type your own name.
4. In **Email**, type your email address.
5. In **Password**, type a password with at least 8 characters.
6. In **Confirm Password**, type the same password again.
7. Click **Create account & organisation**.
8. You may see a message saying **Check your email!** Open your email inbox, find the message from the app, and click the confirmation link inside it.
9. After confirming, the app will guide you through first-time setup (see Chapter 2).

## How to join by invitation

1. An admin sends you an invite. Check your email for it, or ask them for the invite link.
2. Click the invite link. A page opens that says **You've been invited!**
3. If you are new: type your **Full Name**, choose a **Password** (at least 8 characters), type it again in **Confirm Password**, then click **Create account**.
4. If you already have an account: the page will say **Account already exists**. Just type your password and click **Sign in & accept invitation**.
5. You will be taken to the Dashboard. You are now part of the organisation.

## I forgot my password — what do I do?

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot your password?**
2. Type your email or username in the box.
3. Click **Send reset link**.
4. Open your email inbox and click the link in the message you receive.
5. A page opens that says **Set a new password**. Type a new password (at least 8 characters) in both boxes.
6. Click **Set new password**. The app will say **Password updated!** and take you to the Dashboard.

## What you'll see after signing in

- The **Dashboard** with a greeting like "Good morning" and your organisation's money summary.
- A menu on the left (on a computer) or a row of buttons at the bottom (on a phone).
- A **Help Center** button at the bottom of the left menu — that's where this tutorial lives!

## If something goes wrong

- **"Invalid credentials"** — your email or password is wrong. Try again slowly, or use **Forgot your password?**
- **"This link appears invalid or has expired"** — your reset or invite link is too old. Ask for a new one.
- **The invite says it's for a different email** — sign out first, then click the invite link again.
- Still stuck? Contact your organisation administrator.
