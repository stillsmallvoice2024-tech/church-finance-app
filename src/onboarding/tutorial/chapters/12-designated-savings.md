## What is this chapter about?

Three pages that track special pockets of money: **Designated Gifts** (money given for one purpose), **Savings Funds** (money set aside as a reserve), and **Upcoming Deductions** (approved payments that haven't left the bank yet).

## Part 1: Designated Gifts

Find it at **Budget & Allocation → Designated Gifts**.

A designated gift is money with a sticky note on it: "use this for the building project". It is restricted — it should only be spent on that purpose.

### How to read the page

1. The big card at the top shows the **Total Designated Gifts** for the year.
2. Below it, one card per category. Each card lists its **targets** — the purposes people gave for — with how many gifts, the latest date, and the total.
3. Use the search box to find a category, and **Export** to download a spreadsheet.

### How does money get here?

When an inflow's **Fund Type** is set to **Designated Gift** (also called Specific Seed), or a distribution rule sends part of an inflow to the Specific Seed portion. The **Designated Purpose** box on the inflow becomes the target name.

## Part 2: Savings Funds

Find it at **Budget & Allocation → Savings Funds**.

Savings are the organisation's rainy-day money. Each time qualifying money comes in, a slice can go to savings (your distribution rule decides the slice).

### How to read the page

1. Three cards: **Total Saved**, **Withdrawn**, and **Net Balance**.
2. The table lists each category with the same three numbers. Red means more was taken out than saved.
3. To take money OUT of savings, record an outflow with Fund Type = **Savings**, or transfer it to another pocket (Chapter 10).

## Part 3: Upcoming Deductions

Find it at **Review & Processing → Upcoming Deductions**.

A pending deduction is a payment that was approved and recorded, but the money hasn't actually left the bank yet — like a cheque that hasn't been cashed. Recording it reserves the money so nobody spends it twice.

### How to mark a payment as actually paid (resolve it)

1. Wait until the money truly leaves the bank (check your bank statement).
2. Find the row on this page.
3. Make sure the row has both a category and a fund type filled in. If not, click the **pencil**, fill **Category** and **Fund Type**, and save.
4. Click the green **Resolve** button on the row.
5. The row disappears from this list — it is now a normal outflow.

### How to resolve many at once

1. Tick the checkboxes of all the rows that have been paid.
2. Click **Resolve selected** in the bar that appears.
3. Rows missing their category or fund type are skipped — the app tells you how many. Fix those with **Edit selected**, then resolve again.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Fill in both stage codes before resolving"** — the row is missing its Category or Fund Type. Edit it first.
- **"No pending deductions"** — great! Everything approved has been paid.
- **A designated gift shows no target** — edit the original inflow and fill the **Designated Purpose** box.
