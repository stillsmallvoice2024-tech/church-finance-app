## What is this chapter about?

Two admin-only pages: **User Management** (who can use the app and what they may do) and **Activity History** (a diary of every change anyone made). Find them at **Administration → User Management** and **Administration → Activity History**.

## The four roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including transferring ownership and deleting the organisation. There is only one Owner. |
| Admin | Everything except ownership transfer: setup, imports, edits, team management |
| Accountant | Import statements and add/edit transactions. No setup or team management |
| Viewer | Look only. Cannot add, edit, or delete anything |

## How to invite someone

1. Open **User Management** and click **Invite User**.
2. Type their **Email Address**.
3. Pick a **Role**: Admin, Accountant, or Viewer.
4. Click **Send Invitation**.
5. The app emails them automatically AND shows you an **Invite Link**. Click **Copy** and send it to them yourself if the email doesn't arrive. The link works for 7 days.
6. When they accept (Chapter 1), they appear in the **All Members** table.

## How to change someone's role

1. Find the person in the **All Members** table.
2. Click the colored role pill in their row — it's a dropdown.
3. Pick the new role. It saves instantly.
4. You cannot change your own role, and only the Owner can touch other people's Owner status.

## How to remove someone

1. Find them in the table.
2. Click the red **Remove** button in their row.
3. Confirm. They immediately lose access to your organisation.

## How to hand over ownership (Owner only)

1. Find the new owner in the table.
2. Click the purple **Make Owner** button.
3. Confirm. They become the Owner; you become an Admin.

## The Danger Zone (Owner only)

At the bottom is **Delete this organisation**. This locks the organisation immediately for everyone and permanently deletes ALL data after 30 days. The app forces you through several confirmations, makes you re-enter your password, type the organisation's name, and downloads a full backup automatically before submitting. Within the 30 days, the Owner can still restore it. Use with extreme caution.

## Activity History

This page is the app's diary. Every edit anyone makes is written down: when, who, which table, which field, the old value (red) and the new value (green).

### How to investigate a change

1. Open **Activity History**.
2. Use the **Table** dropdown to pick the kind of record (example: Inflow Transactions).
3. Set the **From** and **To** dates around when you think it happened.
4. Use the search box to find the description or value.
5. Read the row: it tells you exactly who changed what, and from what to what.

## If something goes wrong

- **The invite email never arrived** — copy the invite link from the success screen and send it by WhatsApp or any messenger.
- **"Email delivery failed"** — same answer: share the link manually.
- **You can't see these pages** — they are admin-only. Ask an Admin or the Owner.
