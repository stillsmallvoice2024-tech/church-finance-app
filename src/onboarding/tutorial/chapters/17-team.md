## How to Invite a Team Member

**What you'll need:** The new member's email address and the role you want them to have.

**Where to go:** Administration → User Management

**Time:** ~2 minutes.

---

### Steps

1. Open **Administration → User Management** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **Invite User**.
3. Type the person's **Email Address**.
4. Choose a **Role**:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Admin | Everything except ownership transfer: setup, imports, edits, and team management |
| Accountant | Import statements and add / edit transactions — no setup or team access |
| Viewer | Read-only access to all financial data |

5. Click **Send Invitation**.
6. The system emails them automatically and also shows you an **Invite Link**. Copy the link and send it via WhatsApp or any messenger in case the email is delayed or filtered to spam.
7. The link is valid for **7 days**. If it expires, repeat the steps above to resend.

![Screenshot: the Invite User panel with an email filled in and a role selected](./screenshots/17-team-step-07.png)

### Result

The invited person receives a secure link. Once they create their account and log in, they appear in the **All Members** table and have access according to their assigned role.

---

### Common issues

- **Invitation email never arrived:** Use the Invite Link shown after sending and share it directly — the invitation is still valid.
- **"Email delivery failed":** Same resolution — copy and share the link manually.
- **User Management not visible in the menu:** Only Admins and Owners can manage team members.

## How to Change a Member's Role

**Where to go:** Administration → User Management

### Steps

1. Find the person in the **All Members** table.
2. Click the coloured role pill in their row — it is a dropdown.
3. Pick the new role. It saves instantly.

**Notes:**
- You cannot change your own role.
- Only the Owner can change another member's Owner status.

---

## How to Remove a Member

1. Find them in the **All Members** table.
2. Click the red **Remove** button in their row.
3. Confirm. They immediately lose access to the organisation.

---

## How to Hand Over Ownership (Owner only)

1. Find the new owner in the **All Members** table.
2. Click the purple **Make Owner** button.
3. Confirm. They become the Owner; you become an Admin.

---

## How to Investigate a Change in Activity History

**Where to go:** Administration → Activity History

### Steps

1. Open **Administration → Activity History**.
2. Use the **Table** dropdown to filter by record type (e.g. "Inflow Transactions").
3. Set the **From** and **To** dates around when you think the change happened.
4. Use the search box to find a description or value.
5. Read the row — it shows exactly who made the change, which field was edited, and the old and new values.
