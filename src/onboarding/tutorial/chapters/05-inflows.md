## What is the Inflows page?

Inflows are money coming IN — tithes, offerings, donations, and any other income. This page lists every inflow so you can search, check, fix, and export them. Find it in the left menu: **Daily Finance → Inflows**.

Remember: most transactions arrive here through **Import** (Chapter 4). This page is mainly for viewing and fixing.

## What's on the page

- Title: **Inflow Transactions** with buttons **Add Inflow**, **Import**, and **Export**.
- A **filter card** with quick date buttons (Today, This Week, This Month, Last Month, This Year) and **From** / **To** date boxes.
- A **summary strip**: Total (page), Records, Largest, Average.
- A **controls bar**: sort, a **Cards / Table** view switch, a search box ("Search transactions…"), and page size.
- The list itself, with page buttons at the bottom.

## How to find a transaction

1. Type a word in the search box — part of the description, the bank name, or the reference.
2. Or click a quick date button like **This Month** to narrow the dates.
3. Or set exact dates in **From** and **To**.
4. Click **Clear** to remove all filters.

## How to read the table

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| Date | The day the money arrived at the bank |
| Recorded | The day it was typed into the app |
| Bank | Which bank account received it |
| Type | Its tag: an income type, Refund, Reversal, Bank Deposit, etc. |
| Description | What the money was for |
| Amount | How much, shown in green with a + |

Click the small arrow at the left of a row to expand it and see every detail, including its **Transaction Story** (a history of all edits).

## How to edit one inflow

1. Find the row and click the **pencil** (edit) button.
2. The **Edit Inflow Transaction** window opens. Change what you need — date, amount, description, bank, income type, category, fund type, and more.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

## How to edit many inflows at once (bulk edit)

1. In Table view, tick the checkbox at the left of each row you want to change (or the box in the header to select the whole page).
2. A bar appears showing how many are selected. Click **Edit selected**.
3. In the window, fill ONLY the boxes you want to change (anything left as "Keep existing" stays the same).
4. Click **Apply to N record(s)**.

## How to delete inflows

1. Tick the rows, then click **Delete selected** (or use the trash button on a single row).
2. Confirm. Careful — deleting cannot be undone.

## How to export

1. Click **Export** at the top right.
2. Choose **Current Page** (just what you see) or **All Records**.
3. A spreadsheet file downloads to your computer.

## If something goes wrong

- **"No transactions match your filters."** — your search or dates are too narrow. Click **Clear**.
- **You don't see edit or delete buttons** — your role is Viewer (look-only).
- **An amount is wrong** — edit the row; every change is saved in the Transaction Story so nothing is lost.
